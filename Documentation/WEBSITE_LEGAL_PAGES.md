## 5. Acceptable Use Policy

**Page:** `/legal/acceptable-use` | **Last updated:** [DATE]

PenflowAI is a security testing tool. Used properly it protects people. Used wrongly it can harm them and may be a crime. By using it you agree to follow these rules.

### 5.1 You must
- Test only systems you own or have **written** permission to test.
- Prove ownership of each target when we ask (for example by adding a DNS record).
- Upload only genuine, unaltered permission (ROE) documents.
- Keep tests inside the approved targets and time window.
- Tell us quickly if you find a problem in PenflowAI or your account has been misused.

### 5.2 You must not
- Test any system without authorisation, or any system outside your approved scope.
- Forge, edit or misrepresent an ROE or ownership proof, or try to get around the ROE check, the Scope Guard, scope token, rate limits or any other safety control.
- Target government, military, emergency, health-care, financial-infrastructure or other critical systems, or shared systems you do not control, unless you can show clear written authority [DECIDE and enforce with a blocklist].
- Run attacks meant to take a system offline, steal or destroy data, hold data for ransom, or cause damage.
- Use the results to attack, extort, harass or blackmail anyone.
- Share your account, resell access, or use the service to build a competing tool.
- Upload malware or illegal content, or content you have no right to share.
- Overload or probe PenflowAI itself, or attempt to access other users' data.

### 5.3 What happens if you break these rules
We may stop tests, suspend or delete your account without notice and without refund, keep logs as evidence, tell affected parties, and cooperate with law enforcement where required by law.

### 5.4 Report misuse
If you think PenflowAI has been used against your systems, or you found a problem in it, email [ABUSE EMAIL] (see also `/security`).
