## 3.1 What cookies are
Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser.

## 3.2 What we use
We use **only essential cookies**. They are needed for the site to work and cannot be switched off. We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or social media tracking cookies. [CONFIRM — change this page if you add any]

| Cookie name (example — match your real names) | Purpose | Lasts | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pf_session` | Keeps you logged in | Until you log out or [N] hours | Essential, first-party |
| `pf_csrf` | Protects forms against forged requests | Session | Essential, first-party |
| `pf_prefs` (only if you store any choice, e.g. cookie notice dismissed or language) | Remembers a setting you chose | [N] months | Essential/preference, first-party |

## 3.3 Third-party content
We do not load third-party fonts, videos, maps, chat tools or social buttons. Our survey is on another website (Jotform), which has its own cookie rules.

## 3.4 Your choices
Because we only use essential cookies, there is no cookie choice to make. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but you will not be able to log in.

## 3.5 If this changes
If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will first ask for your permission with a banner that has equally clear **Accept** and **Reject** buttons, and we will update this page.
